Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Labs. We were founded in 2014 in Lalitpur and build analytics software for hydropower operators across South Asia. This handbook explains how we work, what you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. It applies to all full-time employees. Contractors should refer to their individual agreements.

Working Hours

Our standard work week runs from Sunday to Friday, with Saturday off. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Nepal time. During core hours you should be available for meetings and respond to messages within a reasonable time. Outside core hours you may arrange your day flexibly, and you can start work at any time between 8:00 and 10:30. Please record your hours in the time tracker by the end of each week.

Remote Work

After completing the 90-day probation period, employees may work up to three remote days per week. Remote days must be agreed with your manager at least one day in advance and noted in the team calendar. While working remotely you must be reachable on the team chat during core hours and join meetings with your camera on when presenting.

Every employee receives a home office stipend of NPR 25,000 per year. The stipend covers chairs, monitors and internet upgrades. It does not cover rent, electricity or personal phones. Unused stipend does not roll over.

Leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next fiscal year; any remaining days expire when the fiscal year closes in mid-July. Leave requests of more than three days should be submitted two weeks in advance.

Employees also receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than three consecutive days, a doctor's note is required on your return. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

Parental leave is 98 days for birthing parents and 15 days for other parents. Parental leave can begin up to two weeks before the expected due date. Please inform People Operations at least eight weeks in advance where possible.

Expenses

Business expenses such as client travel, conference tickets and approved software are reimbursed. Submit expense claims within 30 days of purchase through the Ledger portal, with itemized receipts attached. Claims above NPR 50,000 need approval from a department head before they are paid. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Payroll runs on the 25th of each month.

Hiring and Interviews

Our hiring process has three stages. Candidates first have a 30-minute screening call with a recruiter. Next, candidates complete a take-home exercise, which should be returned within five days. The final stage is a panel interview with three interviewers from the hiring team.

Interview slots are offered from Sunday to Thursday between 10:00 and 16:00. Interviewers must submit written feedback within 24 hours of each interview, using the scorecard in the hiring tool. Candidates hear back within ten working days of the final panel, whatever the outcome.

Security

All company laptops use full-disk encryption, and two-factor authentication is required for every internal system. Never share your credentials, including with colleagues or IT staff. If a device is lost or stolen, report lost devices to the IT security team within one hour so access can be revoked. Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a red visitor badge at all times.

Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of NPR 40,000 that can be spent on courses, books and conference tickets. In addition, employees may take two learning days per quarter to study or attend workshops. Share what you learn in the monthly knowledge-sharing session.
